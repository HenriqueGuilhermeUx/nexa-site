Wallet first

The technology stays behind the experience.

You should not need to become a blockchain expert just to use a digital dollar. Nexa Global keeps the wallet and USDC at the center while hiding unnecessary infrastructure complexity.

1. Verify

Confirm residence and identity through the market-specific onboarding flow.

2. Activate

Create your wallet and prove wallet control by signing a non-financial message.

3. Receive

Use your wallet address to receive USDC while additional rails remain disabled until separately approved.

Control by design

Your wallet is not a promise. It is a verifiable address you control.

The pilot links a wallet only after a signed ownership proof. Signing that message does not authorize a payment or transfer. Country eligibility and identity status are checked server-side, and financial execution remains disabled by default.

Availability, limits and future funding methods depend on market eligibility and provider approval. Nexa Global does not offer a local bank account or fiat balance in Phase 1.
