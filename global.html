Built for the Brazil connection

Family. Freelancers. Services. Life back home.

Nexa Global is designed for Brazilians abroad and international users who need a simpler way to pay or send value to people in Brazil.

Nexa User first

The primary route is username-to-username. The recipient stays inside the Nexa ecosystem and can use the Brazil utilities available to their account.

Brazilian bill pay

A Brazilian abroad can keep paying supported bills in Brazil with a scheduled payment experience.

Pix utility

Where enabled, USDC can be converted and settled through the Nexa Brazil payout infrastructure.

Beyond transfers

BTC, ETH and digital gold.

Use USDC to access supported assets inside the same wallet-first experience. Availability depends on jurisdiction, liquidity and product eligibility.

Country-aware onboarding

The app adapts to your country. It does not guess it.

Residence country, identity documents and applicable verification requirements determine the onboarding path. Device location may support risk controls, but it is not the source of truth for eligibility.

Services, limits, supported funding methods and digital assets may vary by country. Nexa Global does not promise local bank accounts or local fiat balances at launch.
